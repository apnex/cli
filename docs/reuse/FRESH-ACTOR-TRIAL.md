## Remaining evidence

The result supports continuation of this complete prototype checkpoint without its originating conversation.
It does not establish isolated reusable fragments: the preserved service inspection still returns the whole simulated document, including the new queue.
State scoping, dependency composition, and collisions remain concrete composition questions.

A comparison with targeted document editing and with repeated construction remains required before claiming workflow advantage.
Those comparisons must share the outcome requirements while retaining each method's preparation, discovery, repair, and handover costs.
The fresh actor also identified broader catalog fault and transfer predicates it did not rerun; their earlier application evidence remains separate from this trial.

Follow-on disposition, 2026-09-09: the [approved comparison](COMPARISON.md) has now executed all three known methods.
Their definition/runtime outcomes passed, while file editing failed the original staged-continuation predicate.
That result leaves equivalent full-workflow economics unresolved and does not change this actor's historical measurements.

## Mechanics, rationale, and consequence

### Mechanics

Keep the full actor account and receiver trace, evaluate the submitted artifacts against frozen tasks, and record context exposure separately from acceptance.

### Rationale

The proposed value is resumable meaning carried by structured work, so the trial must include a new actor interpreting and extending that work.

### Consequence of violation

A successful scripted replay, compact definition, or self-reported completion could otherwise become an unsupported claim about agent independence or workflow savings.
