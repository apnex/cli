## Mechanics, rationale, and consequence

### Mechanics

Follow source intent to proposed responsibilities, then to the unresolved contract relevant to the task.
Measure existing behavior through available checks before claiming that a proposal is implemented.

### Rationale

This guide gives another actor a usable starting point without treating conversation memory as a dependency.

### Consequence of violation

Resuming from directory names alone can turn scaffolding choices into unstated requirements and erase the workflow value the project exists to deliver.
