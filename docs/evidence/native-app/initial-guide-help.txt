agp
Inspect a local AGP node. Enter a context, then use show or ls.
  advertisements/  Received advertisements and their source sessions.
  configuration/  Effective redacted node configuration.
  connections/  Display connection identities, states, supplied uptime and hold TTL.
  counters/  Canonical operational counters.
  endpoints/  Locally registered endpoints and binding identities.
  forwarding/  Resolved forwarding entries.
  health/  Node lifecycle, readiness, and identity.
  resources/  Resource usage and observed high-water marks.
  routes/  Display route candidates and supplied selected-route membership.
  snapshot/  Summary of one canonical operations capture; --json retains all sections.
